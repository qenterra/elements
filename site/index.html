The article you actually came to read

That banner above? On a real page you'd hover it, press Space, and it would be gone — on every visit.

A precision tool, not a hack

Hide anything

Banners, popups, sidebars, comment sections — select and press Space.

Edit text in place

Fix a typo, rename a heading, rewrite a label — right on the page.

Restyle blocks

Round corners, blur, dim, or desaturate any element. Custom CSS for power users.

Remembered per site

Rules re-apply on every visit and may sync through your browser account. Pause or undo any time.

Light and dark

Follows your system theme, or pick one. The picker looks at home on any page.

Keyboard-first

Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+X to open, Q/W to walk the DOM, one key per action, full undo/redo.

Private by design

No analytics. No ads. No remote code. No developer-operated server. Rules stay in browser-managed extension storage and may be processed by your browser's sync service; the developer does not receive them. The code is MIT-licensed and open — read it, build it, own it.
