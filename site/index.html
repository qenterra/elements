The useful parts

A page editor that stays out of your way.

Open the picker only when you need it. Your edits remain reviewable, reversible, and scoped to the site where you made them.

Hide the noise

Remove banners, sticky bars, sidebars, comment blocks, and other visual clutter.

Rewrite visible text

Rename a label, fix a typo, or replace a heading without touching the source page.

Restyle the page

Round, blur, dim, desaturate, or add custom CSS when the built-in actions are not enough.

Undo the whole story

Undo and redo full edits, inspect saved rules, pause them, or remove them individually.

Move through layers

Use Q and W to walk up and down the DOM while the active breadcrumb stays in view.

Keep changes per site

Remembered rules return on later visits and can use your browser's sync storage.

Product tour

The real interface, not a decorative mockup.

Choose a view to inspect the picker, the settings hub, or the compact mobile layout.

Private by construction

Your page edits do not pass through our server.

There is no developer-operated backend. Rules stay in extension storage managed by your browser and may use the browser's own sync service if available.

  • No analytics or advertising
  • No remote code in the extension
  • Open source and independently inspectable

For developers and careful people

Small surface area. Inspectable decisions.

Elements is a Chrome Manifest V3 extension built with TypeScript, React, and WXT. The repository includes automated unit, browser, build, documentation, and release checks.
